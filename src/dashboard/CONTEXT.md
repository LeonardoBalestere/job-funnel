# Dashboard

The measurements of the job search. Dashboard derives everything it knows from what happened in Catalog and Funnel.

## Language

**Timeline**:
The history of one Opportunity as Dashboard sees it: which Statuses it reached and when, with the attributes of its Job Posting.

**Stage Reached**:
A Status an Opportunity arrived at, or skipped over on its way forward, not counting voided Transitions.

**Conversion**:
Of the Opportunities that reached a Status, the fraction that reached the next one.

**Response**:
An applied Opportunity that reached interview or offer, or was rejected by the company.
_Avoid_: Reply, feedback

**Response Window**:
The number of days after applying before an Opportunity counts in the Response Rate.

**Response Rate**:
Responses divided by the Opportunities applied to longer ago than the Response Window, per Channel.

**Term Coverage**:
The Coverage of the Terms that Job Postings ask for most.

**Tenant**:
The owner of a set of data: the real owner, or the demonstration data set.
